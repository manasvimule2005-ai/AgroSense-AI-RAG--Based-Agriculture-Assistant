# Integrated Pest Management (IPM) & Biological Crop Protection Manual

*Document Source: Directorate of Plant Protection, Quarantine & Storage (DPPQ&S), Government of India & CABI.*

## 1. Principles of Integrated Pest Management (IPM)
Integrated Pest Management is an ecosystem-based strategy that focuses on long-term prevention of pests through a combination of techniques:
1. **Cultural Controls**: Crop rotation, intercropping, solarization, deep summer ploughing, and balanced fertilization.
2. **Mechanical & Physical Controls**: Hand-picking large larvae, installing light traps, sticky cards, and bird perches.
3. **Biological Controls**: Natural predators (ladybird beetles, chrysoperla), parasitoids (*Trichogramma*), and entomopathogenic fungi (*Beauveria bassiana*, *Metarhizium anisopliae*).
4. **Chemical Controls**: Used strictly as a last resort when the pest density crosses the Economic Threshold Level (ETL).

## 2. Economic Injury Level (EIL) and Economic Threshold Level (ETL)
- **Economic Injury Level (EIL)**: The lowest population density that will cause economic damage exceeding the cost of control measures.
- **Economic Threshold Level (ETL)**: The pest density at which control measures should be initiated to prevent the pest population from reaching the EIL.

### Benchmark ETL Levels for Major Field Pests:
- **Rice Yellow Stem Borer**: 1 egg mass/m² or 5% dead heart in vegetative stage, 1 moth/m² at panicle stage.
- **Rice Brown Planthopper (BPH)**: 5-10 insects per hill during tillering; 15-20 per hill during flowering.
- **Cotton Pink Bollworm**: 8 moths per pheromone trap per night for 3 consecutive nights, or 10% damaged green bolls.
- **Maize Fall Armyworm**: 5% damaged plants in seedling stage; 10% in mid-whorl stage; 20% in late whorl stage.
- **Chickpea Pod Borer (*Helicoverpa armigera*)**: 1 larva per meter row or 2-3 damaged pods per plant.

## 3. Biological & Botanical Control Agents
- **Neem Formulations (Azadirachtin)**:
  - Mode of action: Antifeedant, insect growth regulator (IGR), repellant, and oviposition deterrent.
  - Usage: Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE 5%) or Azadirachtin 10,000 ppm @ 2-3 ml/L of water. Effective against early instars of sucking pests, lepidopteran larvae, and thrips.
- **Trichoderma viride / harzianum**:
  - Application: Soil application (2.5 kg mixed with 100 kg FYM/ha) or seed treatment (10 g/kg seed). Prevents soil-borne root rot, damping off, and vascular wilts.
- **Trichogramma Wasps (Egg Parasitoids)**:
  - Release rate: 100,000 to 150,000 parasitized eggs (Tricho-cards) per hectare in 4-5 weekly releases starting from pest egg detection.
- **Beauveria bassiana**:
  - Entomopathogenic fungus targeting whiteflies, thrips, aphids, and beetles. Apply @ 5 g/L during evening hours with high relative humidity (>70%).
