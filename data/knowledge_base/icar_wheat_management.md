# ICAR Wheat Production, Irrigation Scheduling & Rust Protection Guide

*Document Source: ICAR - Indian Institute of Wheat and Barley Research (IIWBR), Karnal, Extension Bulletin No. 58.*

## 1. Climatic Requirements and Sowing Windows
Wheat (*Triticum aestivum*) is a temperate rabi cereal that requires cool vegetative conditions and dry warm weather during grain maturity.
- **Optimum Temperature**:
  - Germination: 20°C to 25°C.
  - Tillering and vegetative growth: 16°C to 20°C.
  - Grain development and ripening: 21°C to 24°C. High temperatures (>30°C) during grain filling trigger terminal heat stress and shrivel grain.
- **Sowing Window (North-Western Plains Zone - NWPZ)**:
  - Timely Sowing: November 1 to November 15.
  - Recommended Varieties: DBW 187 (Karan Vandana), DBW 303 (Karan Vaishnavi), HD 3226, PBW 824.
  - Late Sowing: November 25 to December 10 (Varieties: HD 3059, PBW 771, DBW 173). Every week delay after November 20 reduces yield by 150-200 kg/ha.

## 2. Soil Suitability and Seed Bed Preparation
- **Soil**: Fertile, well-drained loam and clay-loam soils with neutral pH (6.5 to 7.5). Avoid saline-alkali or waterlogged soils.
- **Tillage**: Conventional tillage with 1 disc harrowing followed by 2 cultivator passes and planking. Alternatively, Zero Tillage (Happy Seeder / Super Seeder) directly into standing rice residues saves water, fuel, and reduces weed pressure.
- **Seed Rate**: 100 kg/ha for timely sown, 125 kg/ha for late sown crops.

## 3. Fertilizer Recommendations and Balanced Nutrition
- **Recommended Doses**:
  - Timely Sown Irrigated: 150 kg N, 60 kg P2O5, 40 kg K2O per hectare.
  - Late Sown: 120 kg N, 50 kg P2O5, 30 kg K2O per hectare.
- **Split Application**:
  - Basal: 1/3rd Nitrogen + 100% Phosphorus + 100% Potassium.
  - First Top Dressing: 1/3rd Nitrogen at 1st irrigation (CRI stage, 21 DAS).
  - Second Top Dressing: 1/3rd Nitrogen at 2nd irrigation (Tillering/Jointing, 40-45 DAS).

## 4. Scientific Irrigation Scheduling (Critical Physiological Stages)
Wheat typically requires 4 to 6 irrigations of 60 mm depth depending on soil texture and winter rainfall.
1. **Crown Root Initiation (CRI)** (20 - 25 DAS): The single most critical irrigation. Delay at this stage impairs tillering and reduces yield by 25-30%.
2. **Tillering Stage** (40 - 45 DAS): Encourages vigorous secondary tillers.
3. **Late Jointing Stage** (60 - 65 DAS): Promotes internode elongation and stem strength.
4. **Flowering / Booting Stage** (80 - 85 DAS): Ensures optimum spikelet fertility and pollen viability.
5. **Milk Stage** (100 - 105 DAS): Critical for grain weight accumulation.
6. **Dough Stage** (115 - 120 DAS): Final moisture support. Avoid irrigating on windy days to prevent lodging.

## 5. Major Disease: Yellow (Stripe) Rust Management
- **Causal Organism**: *Puccinia striiformis f. sp. tritici*.
- **Favorable Weather**: Temperature 10-15°C with intermittent showers and heavy morning dew.
- **Field Identification**: Bright yellow powder/pustules arranged in distinct stripes or linear rows along the veins of leaf blades. Spores easily stain fingers and clothing yellow.
- **Integrated Management**:
  - Cultivate certified rust-resistant varieties (DBW 303, DBW 187, HD 3226).
  - Regular field scouting along borders and field channels.
  - At first detection of yellow stripes, immediately spray Propiconazole 25% EC (Tilt) @ 1.0 ml per liter of water (500 ml in 500 L water/ha) or Tebuconazole 25.9% EC @ 1.0 ml/L. Repeat after 15 days if cloudy weather persists.
