# ICAR Maize (Corn) Cultivation and Disease Management Guide

## Overview
This guide is based on standards from **ICAR-Indian Institute of Maize Research (IIMR), Ludhiana** and covers complete agronomic protocols for *Zea mays L.* under Kharif, Rabi, and Zaid seasons across diverse agroecological zones of India.

---

## 1. Crop Introduction

Maize is India's **third most important cereal crop** after rice and wheat. India cultivates approximately **9.5 million hectares** of maize with a national average productivity of ~3.0 t/ha, while improved management can achieve 7–10 t/ha.

- **Major States:** Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Bihar, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh
- **Season:** Kharif (June–July), Rabi (October–November), Zaid (February–March under irrigation)
- **Uses:** Grain (food, feed, starch), green cob vegetable, silage, popcorn, baby corn

## 3. Soil & Climate

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Soil Type | Well-drained sandy loam to clay loam; Alluvial soils preferred |
| Soil pH | 5.8–7.5 (optimal 6.0–7.0) |
| Soil Depth | Minimum 45 cm |
| Rainfall | 600–900 mm; avoid waterlogging (sensitive at all stages) |
| Temperature | 18–32°C optimal; germination min 10°C |
| Photoperiod | Day-neutral (14-hr optimal for vegetative growth) |

---

## 4. Land Preparation

- 2–3 deep ploughings; ensure fine tilth for uniform germination
- Apply FYM or compost @ 10 t/ha and incorporate well before sowing
- Recommended: Broad bed and furrow (BBF) system to avoid waterlogging

---

## 5. Fertilizer Schedule (Irrigated Kharif, kg/ha)

| Nutrient | Dose (kg/ha) | Application Method |
|---------|-------------|-------------------|
| Nitrogen (N) | 120–150 | 1/3 basal + 1/3 at knee-high (V6) + 1/3 at tasseling (V10) |
| Phosphorus (P₂O₅) | 60–75 | Full as basal SSP/DAP |
| Potassium (K₂O) | 40–60 | Full as basal MOP |
| Zinc (ZnSO₄) | 25 | Basal if Zn deficient (<0.6 ppm) |
| Sulphur (S) | 20 | As SSP at basal |
| Boron | 1.5 | Foliar at VT (tassel emergence) |

**Urea split ratio:** 33:33:34 (basal:V6:V10 growth stages).
**Foliar spray:** 1% Urea at VT stage improves grain protein content.

**Micronutrient deficiency symptoms:**
- **Zinc deficiency:** White striping on young leaves ("white bud"); apply ZnSO₄ 0.5% foliar
- **Iron deficiency:** Interveinal chlorosis on young leaves; apply FeSO₄ 0.5% foliar
- **Boron deficiency:** Failure of silks; poor pollination; apply Borax 0.2%

## 6. Sowing

| Parameter | Recommendation |
|-----------|---------------|
| Sowing time (Kharif) | 15 June – 15 July (after first rains) |
| Sowing time (Rabi) | 15 Oct – 15 Nov (North India) |
| Row spacing | 60 cm × 20 cm (Kharif irrigated) |
| Plant population | 65,000–75,000 plants/ha |
| Seed rate | 18–20 kg/ha (hybrid); 20–25 kg/ha (OPV) |
| Seed depth | 4–5 cm in moist soil |
| Seed treatment | Thiram 75 WS @ 2 g/kg + Azospirillum @ 100 g/bag |

## 7. Irrigation Management

| Growth Stage | Irrigation | Days After Sowing |
|-------------|-----------|------------------|
| Germination | Pre-sowing irrigation | -3 to 0 |
| Vegetative (V4) | 1st irrigation | 25–30 DAS |
| Knee-high (V6) | Critical | 40–45 DAS |
| Tasseling/Silking | Most critical | 55–65 DAS |
| Grain fill (R3) | Critical | 75–90 DAS |
| Grain maturity (R6) | Withhold | >100 DAS |

**Critical stages:** Tasseling and silking (10 days) is the **most drought-sensitive period** — even 2–3 days of water deficit can cause 30–50% yield loss through poor pollination.

**Total water requirement:** 500–650 mm; irrigation interval 10–12 days.

## 8. Major Pests of Maize — IPM Protocols

### 8.1 Fall Armyworm (FAW) — Spodoptera frugiperda (Invasive Pest since 2018)
- **Symptoms:** Ragged feeding on whorls; "windowpane" feeding; large circular holes; "frass" (sawdust-like excrement) in whorls; damaged growing point (dead heart)
- **ETL:** 1 larva/plant at V1–V4; 2 larvae/plant at V5–V8
- **Scouting:** Early morning; check whorl for larvae; count damaged plants (>5% = action)
- **Cultural:** Deep ploughing to destroy pupae; summer tillage; intercropping with soybean
- **Biological:**
  - *Trichogramma* egg parasitoids @ 50,000/ha/week (start at first instar)
  - *Metarhizium anisopliae* @ 5×10⁸ cfu/ml (soil drench or spray)
  - *Beauveria bassiana* @ 5 g/L (spray into whorl)
  - Spodoptera frugiperda NPV (SfNPV) @ 1×10¹² POB/ha
- **Chemical (whorl spray):**
  - Emamectin benzoate 5 SG @ 0.4 g/L water (whorl spray; best efficacy)
  - Chlorantraniliprole 18.5 SC @ 0.3 ml/L (systemic; moderate resistance)
  - Spinetoram 11.7 SC @ 0.5 ml/L (alternation group)
  - Thiamethoxam 12.6% + Lambda-cyhalothrin 9.5% ZC @ 0.5 ml/L
- **Resistance management:** Rotate between IRAC Groups 1A, 5, 28 each season

### 8.2 Stem Borer (Chilo partellus, Sesamia inferens)
- **Symptoms:** Dead hearts in young plants (whorl stage); "pinhole" on leaves; hollow stems; deadheart
- **ETL:** 5–10% deadheart incidence OR 1 larva/10 plants
- **Cultural:** Remove and destroy deadhearts; avoid ratoon cropping
- **Biological:** *Trichogramma chilonis* @ 50,000/ha at 10 and 25 DAS; *Cotesia flavipes* parasitoid
- **Chemical:** Carbofuran 3G @ 0.3 g/plant (whorl application) OR Fipronil 0.3 GR @ 0.3 g/plant

### 8.3 Maize Aphid (Rhopalosiphum maidis)
- **Symptoms:** Colony on tassels/leaves; honeydew + sooty mould; reduces photosynthesis
- **ETL:** 50 aphids/plant
- **Control:** Dimethoate 30 EC @ 2 ml/L; encourage natural enemies (ladybird beetles)

### 8.4 Shoot Fly (Atherigona soccata)
- **Symptoms:** Wilting of central shoot ("deadheart") in seedlings 7–21 DAS
- **Management:** Seed treatment with Imidacloprid 48% FS @ 7.5 ml/kg seed

## 9. Major Diseases of Maize — Management Protocols

### 9.1 Northern Corn Leaf Blight (NCLB) — Exserohilum turcicum
- **Symptoms:** Large, elliptical, tan-grey lesions (10–15 cm) with dark borders on leaves; "cigar-shaped" lesions
- **Favourable Conditions:** Cool temperatures (18–27°C), high humidity (>80%), prolonged leaf wetness
- **Management:**
  - Resistant hybrids: 900 M Gold, PAC 740 (partial resistance)
  - Mancozeb 75 WP @ 2.5 g/L at first sign + repeat at 15 days
  - Propiconazole 25 EC @ 1 ml/L at tasseling stage

### 9.2 Maize Stalk Rot (Pythium, Fusarium complex)
- **Symptoms:** Lower stem nodes show discolouration; plant lodges easily; roots decay; pith becomes shredded
- **Favourable Conditions:** High N, low K, water stress at grain fill, dense plant population
- **Management:**
  - Balanced fertilization (maintain N:K ratio of 3:2)
  - Trichoderma seed treatment (4 g/kg)
  - Avoid excessive plant density; ensure proper drainage
  - Metalaxyl-M + Mancozeb (Ridomil Gold) @ 2 g/L as soil drench if severe

### 9.3 Common Rust (Puccinia sorghi)
- **Symptoms:** Small, circular to elongate, golden-brown pustules on both leaf surfaces; urediniospores (brick-red to cinnamon)
- **Favourable:** Cool weather (15–20°C), high humidity
- **Management:**
  - Resistant hybrids preferred
  - Mancozeb 75 WP @ 2.5 g/L or Propiconazole 25 EC @ 1 ml/L at R2–R3 stage

### 9.4 Downy Mildew (Peronosclerospora sorghi)
- **Symptoms:** Systemic infection; white downy growth on underside of leaves; "crazy top" symptom (proliferation of tassel into leaf-like structures); plants stunted
- **Management:**
  - Treat seed with Metalaxyl 35 WS @ 6 g/kg seed
  - Remove and destroy infected plants (systemic disease; no chemical cure after infection)
  - Use resistant hybrids from certified sources

### 9.5 Silking/Ear Rots (Gibberella, Aspergillus, Fusarium)
- **Symptoms:** Pinkish-red mould at silk end of ear (Gibberella); grey-green mould (Aspergillus — aflatoxin risk); white cottony growth (Fusarium)
- **High-risk condition:** Prolonged wet weather at pollination; insect damage creates entry points
- **Management:**
  - Harvest at correct maturity (husk colour change, kernel black layer)
  - Dry to <13% moisture immediately
  - Captan 50 WP @ 2.5 g/L foliar at silking if Aspergillus risk is high
  - **Food safety alert:** Do NOT feed Aspergillus-infected grain to livestock (aflatoxin B1 risk)

## 10. Harvesting & Storage

- **Physiological maturity:** 100–110 DAS (formation of black layer at kernel base)
- **Moisture at harvest:** 25–35% (cob harvest); 20–22% (shelling)
- **Drying:** Reduce to 12–13% before storage using mechanical dryers or solar drying
- **Storage:** Hermetic storage bags (IRRI SuperGrain bags) for small farmers; prevent aflatoxin by keeping moisture < 13%
- **Expected yield:** 5–8 t/ha grain (irrigated hybrid); 2.5–4 t/ha (rain-fed OPV)

---

## 11. Baby Corn & Sweet Corn Specific Notes

**Baby Corn:**
- Harvest when silk has emerged 2–3 cm (1–3 days after silk emergence)
- Harvest every alternate day for 10–12 days
- Remove husk before marketing; store at 0–4°C with high humidity
- Yield: 10–12 t (unhusked) = 2.5–3 t (husked baby corn cobs)

**Sweet Corn:**
- Harvest at "milk stage" (R3) when kernels contain 70–75% moisture
- Use thumb-nail test: milky juice = harvest
- Refrigerate immediately after harvest (sugar converts to starch rapidly)
- Shelf life: 2–4 days at 4°C; 1–2 days at ambient
