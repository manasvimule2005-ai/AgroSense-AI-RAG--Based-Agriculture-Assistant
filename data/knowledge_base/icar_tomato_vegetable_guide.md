# ICAR Vegetable Cultivation Guide: Tomato Production, Drip Fertigation & Blight Control

*Document Source: ICAR - Indian Institute of Horticultural Research (IIHR), Bengaluru, Extension Guide No. 22.*

## 1. Soil and Climate Requirements
- **Crop**: Tomato (*Solanum lycopersicum*).
- **Climate**: Warm-season vegetable sensitive to frost and extreme heat. Optimal day temperature is 21-28°C and night temperature 15-20°C.
- **Pollination & Fruit Set**: When temperatures exceed 35°C or drop below 13°C, pollen viability drops drastically, leading to flower drop and poor fruit setting.
- **Soil**: Deep, well-drained sandy loam or clay loam with high organic matter. pH 6.0 - 7.0.

## 2. Nursery Management and Transplanting
- **Pro-tray Nursery**: Raise seedlings in 98-cell plastic pro-trays using sterilized coco peat + vermicompost + *Trichoderma harzianum* (1:1:0.05). Seedlings are ready for transplanting in 25-30 days (4 true leaves).
- **Hardening Off**: Withhold irrigation 3-4 days before field transplanting to induce root hardiness.
- **Spacing**:
  - Determinate (bush) varieties: 60 cm x 45 cm.
  - Indeterminate (staking) hybrids: 90-120 cm row-to-row and 45-60 cm plant-to-plant on raised beds (1 m top width, 15 cm height).
- **Mulching**: Silver-black polyethylene mulch film (25-30 micron) controls weeds, conserves 40% moisture, and repels sucking pests.

## 3. Water Management & Drip Fertigation Schedule
Over-head sprinkler irrigation promotes fungal foliar infections like Early Blight and Late Blight. Drip irrigation is strongly advised.
- **Water Consumption**: 600 - 800 mm during crop cycle.
- **Blossom End Rot (BER)**:
  - Symptoms: Water-soaked, sunken black patch at the blossom end of fruits.
  - Cause: Calcium deficiency caused by fluctuating moisture stress, not simply low soil calcium.
  - Remediation: Maintain regular, uniform drip irrigation; spray Calcium Nitrate @ 2-3 g/L during fruit expansion.
- **Fruit Cracking**:
  - Cause: Heavy irrigation or rain following a dry spell causing rapid expansion of fruit pulp faster than the epidermis.
  - Remediation: Maintain uniform soil moisture; apply Boron (Solubor @ 1 g/L) at flowering.

## 4. Major Pest and Disease Protocols
- **Early Blight (*Alternaria solani*)**:
  - Symptoms: Brown to black spots with concentric rings ("target board") on older leaves.
  - Control: Spray Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L or Chlorothalonil 75% WP @ 2 g/L at 10-day intervals.
- **Late Blight (*Phytophthora infestans*)**:
  - Symptoms: Water-soaked dark green/black lesions on leaves with white cottony mold on the underside in cool, foggy weather.
  - Control: Spray Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2.5 g/L or Cymoxanil 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2 g/L.
- **Tomato Leaf Miner (*Tuta absoluta*)**:
  - Symptoms: Blotch mines on leaves; larvae tunnel into green and ripe fruits.
  - Control: Install pheromone traps (16/ha). Spray Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 0.3 ml/L or Spinosad 45% SC @ 0.3 ml/L.
