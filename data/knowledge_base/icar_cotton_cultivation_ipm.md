# ICAR Cotton Cultivation and IPM Management Guide

## Overview
This guide is based on standards from the **Central Institute for Cotton Research (ICAR-CICR), Nagpur** and State Agricultural Universities, covering the complete agronomic calendar for *Gossypium hirsutum* (American/Bt Cotton) and *Gossypium arboreum* (Desi Cotton) across rain-fed and irrigated conditions in India.

---

## 1. Crop Introduction & Importance

Cotton is the **Golden Fibre** of India and the most important commercial crop. India is the world's largest producer of cotton with approximately **12.5 million hectares** under cultivation across Maharashtra, Gujarat, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka, Punjab, and Haryana.

- **Season:** Kharif (June–July sowing; October–February harvesting)
- **Varieties:** Bt hybrids (NHH 44 Bt, MRC 7017 Bt), Non-Bt (LD 327, Suvin)
- **Yield Potential:** 25–35 quintals lint/ha (irrigated Bt hybrids)

---

## 2. Soil & Climate Requirements

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Soil Type | Deep black cotton soil (Vertisols), sandy loam, clay loam |
| Soil pH | 6.5–8.0 (tolerates mild alkalinity) |
| Soil Depth | Minimum 1 meter for tap root penetration |
| Rainfall | 500–1000 mm well-distributed; critical at flowering and boll development |
| Temperature | 21–30°C optimal; germination needs >18°C |
| Altitude | Up to 1000 m MSL |

**Avoid:** Waterlogged soils, soils with hard pan within 30 cm, highly acidic or sodic soils.

---

## 3. Land Preparation & Fertilizer Schedule

### Primary Tillage
- 1 deep ploughing (20–25 cm) followed by 2 cross harrowings
- Apply **FYM 10 tonnes/ha** or compost before final land preparation

### Fertilizer Recommendation (Irrigated Bt Cotton, kg/ha)
| Nutrient | Dose | Application |
|---------|------|-------------|
| Nitrogen (N) | 150–180 kg/ha | In 4 splits: 25% basal, 25% at squaring, 25% at first flower, 25% at boll setting |
| Phosphorus (P₂O₅) | 60–80 kg/ha | Full dose as basal SSP/DAP |
| Potassium (K₂O) | 60 kg/ha | 50% basal + 50% at squaring |
| Sulphur (S) | 20 kg/ha | As SSP or Bentonite S at basal |
| Zinc (ZnSO₄) | 25 kg/ha | As basal if soil Zn < 0.6 ppm |
| Boron | 1.5 kg/ha foliar | At bud initiation + boll formation |

**Foliar nutrition:** 2% KNO₃ at boll development to improve fibre quality.

---

## 4. Sowing & Crop Establishment

- **Sowing window:** June 1st week to July 1st week (onset of monsoon)
- **Row spacing:** 90×30 cm (irrigated); 60×30 cm (rain-fed desi cotton)
- **Seed rate:** Bt hybrid: 450–600 g/ha; Desi: 10–12 kg/ha
- **Seed treatment:** Imidacloprid 48% FS @ 7.5 ml/kg + Trichoderma viride @ 4 g/kg
- **Germination:** 7–10 days at >25°C; replant gaps within 15 days

---

## 5. Water Management (Irrigation Schedule)

| Growth Stage | Critical Irrigation | Frequency |
|-------------|--------------------|-----------| 
| Germination (0–10 DAS) | Pre-sowing or first rain | At sowing |
| Squaring (45–60 DAS) | High critical stage | Every 10–12 days |
| First Flowering (60–90 DAS) | Most critical | Every 8–10 days |
| Boll Setting (90–120 DAS) | Critical for yield | Every 10 days |
| Boll Maturation (>120 DAS) | Reduce irrigation | Every 15 days |

**Total water requirement:** 700–900 mm for irrigated crop.
**Drip irrigation:** Saves 40–50% water; apply 4–6 L/plant/day during flowering.

## 6. Integrated Pest Management (IPM) for Cotton

### 6.1 Pink Bollworm (Pectinophora gossypiella)
- **Symptoms:** Circular entrance holes in bolls; rosette flowers; seeds hollowed
- **Economic Threshold Level (ETL):** >5% rosette flowers or 10% damaged bolls
- **Biological Control:** Release *Trichogramma* egg parasitoids @ 1,00,000/ha at 50 DAS; install pheromone traps (5/ha)
- **Chemical Control:** Profenofos 50 EC @ 2 ml/L or Emamectin benzoate 5 SG @ 0.4 g/L

### 6.2 Bollworm Complex (Helicoverpa armigera, Spodoptera frugiperda)
- **Symptoms:** Circular holes in bolls; shed flower buds; green semi-circular marks on bolls
- **ETL:** 1 larva per plant at bud/flowering stage
- **Cultural:** Deep ploughing to destroy pupae; American strain Bt cotton has in-built resistance
- **Biological:** *Beauveria bassiana* @ 5 g/L water; NPV (Helicoverpa NPV) @ 1.5×10¹² POB/ha
- **Chemical (rotation):** Spinosad 45 SC @ 0.3 ml/L; Chlorantraniliprole 18.5 SC @ 0.3 ml/L

### 6.3 Whitefly (Bemisia tabaci) — Vector of Cotton Leaf Curl Virus (CLCuV)
- **Symptoms:** Yellowing, leaf curling, sooty mould, honeydew secretion
- **ETL:** 20 nymphs/leaf or 5 adults/leaf
- **Cultural:** Avoid late sowing; plant resistant varieties; remove volunteer plants
- **Biological:** *Encarsia formosa* parasitoid releases; neem-based insecticides
- **Chemical:** Flonicamid 50 WG @ 0.3 g/L; Spiromesifen 240 SC @ 1 ml/L (avoid pyrethroids)
- **Note:** Rotate between IRAC Group 9 and Group 23 to prevent resistance

### 6.4 Thrips (Thrips tabaci)
- **Symptoms:** Silvery leaf sheen; crinkling of leaves; heavy infestation stunts crop
- **ETL:** 10 thrips/leaf
- **Chemical:** Spinosad 45 SC @ 0.3 ml/L or Fipronil 5 SC @ 1.5 ml/L

### 6.5 Sucking Pests Complex — Aphids, Jassids, Mites
- **Control:** Dimethoate 30 EC @ 2 ml/L (aphids); Propargite 57 EC @ 2 ml/L (mites)
- **Avoid:** Broad-spectrum insecticides that eliminate natural enemies (predatory beetles, spiders)

### 6.6 Root Rot (Rhizoctonia bataticola) & Fusarium Wilt
- **Symptoms:** Yellowing from base; root turns brown/dark; plant wilts suddenly
- **Management:** Trichoderma viride seed treatment (4 g/kg) + soil application (2 kg/ha with FYM)

---

## 7. Defoliation & Harvesting

- **Harvesting:** 160–180 DAS; bolls open naturally in 3–4 picks spaced 15–20 days apart
- **Defoliation:** Chemical defoliation with Thidiazuron (Dropp) @ 100–200 g/ha 7–10 days before final harvest to ease machine picking
- **Quality factors:** Moisture content <12%; avoid mixing of grades during picking

---

## 8. Post-Harvest Soil Management

- Incorporate cotton stalks into soil by deep chiselling (source of K)
- Avoid burning crop residues (destroys 80% surface soil organic matter)
- Plant green manure crop (Dhaincha) in inter-season
