# ICAR Package of Practices: Rice (Paddy) Cultivation & Water Management

*Document Source: ICAR - Indian Institute of Rice Research (IIRR), Hyderabad, Technical Bulletin No. 44.*

## 1. Agro-Climatic Requirements
Rice is a tropical and subtropical cereal that thrives in warm, humid conditions.
- **Optimum Temperature**: 22°C to 32°C. Day temperatures exceeding 35°C during anthesis (flowering) cause spikelet sterility.
- **Soil Requirements**: Heavy clay or clay loam soils capable of retaining water with pH between 5.5 and 7.0. Vertisols and alluvial soils are ideal.
- **Water Requirement**: 1100 mm to 1500 mm across the crop life cycle.

## 2. Seed Bed Preparation and Nursery Management
- **Seed Treatment**: Soak seeds in salt water (specific gravity 1.06) to discard floating unviable seeds. Treat healthy seeds with *Pseudomonas fluorescens* or *Trichoderma viride* @ 10 g/kg seed, or Carbendazim @ 2 g/kg seed to prevent seed-borne blast and brown spot.
- **Wet Nursery Method**: Allocate 1000 m² nursery area per hectare of main field. Apply 10 kg Nitrogen, 10 kg P2O5, and 10 kg K2O per 1000 m².
- **Seedling Age**: Transplant seedlings at 20-25 days old (4th leaf stage) for medium duration varieties. Over-aged seedlings result in reduced tillering.

## 3. Main Field Preparation & Nutrient Management
- **Puddling**: Plough twice dry followed by two wet ploughings and leveling. Puddling reduces water percolation losses, controls weeds, and facilitates easy transplanting.
- **Recommended Fertilizer Doses (NPK)**:
  - Medium Duration Varieties: 120:60:60 kg N:P2O5:K2O per hectare.
  - Hybrids: 150:75:75 kg N:P2O5:K2O per hectare.
- **Application Schedule**:
  - Nitrogen: Apply 50% as basal, 25% at active tillering (20-25 DAT), and 25% at panicle initiation (PI) stage.
  - Phosphorus: 100% as basal during final leveling.
  - Potassium: 50% basal and 50% at panicle initiation.
  - Zinc Deficiency ("Khaira Disease"): Apply Zinc Sulphate heptahydrate @ 25 kg/ha basally or foliar spray 0.5% ZnSO4 + 0.25% lime when symptoms (brown pigmentation on middle leaves) appear.

## 4. Water Management & Critical Stages
Continuous submerged ponding is not necessary and causes water wastage and greenhouse gas (methane) emissions.
- **Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD)**: Maintain 2-5 cm shallow water layer up to 7 days after transplanting for root establishment. Thereafter, allow ponded water to subside until water table drops to 15 cm below soil surface (monitored using a perforated PVC field tube) before re-flooding.
- **Critical Stages for Water**:
  1. Active Tillering (20 to 35 DAT).
  2. Panicle Initiation (45 to 60 DAT).
  3. Flowering and Anthesis (70 to 85 DAT).
  Water stress during flowering causes empty spikelets (chaffy grain) and severe yield penalty. Drain field 10 to 12 days prior to harvest.

## 5. Major Pest & Disease Management
- **Yellow Stem Borer (*Scirpophaga incertulas*)**:
  - Symptoms: Dead hearts in vegetative stage; White ears during reproductive stage.
  - Control: Release egg parasitoid *Trichogramma japonicum* @ 100,000/ha. If ETL exceeded (5% dead heart or 1 moth/m²), spray Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 150 ml/ha or apply Cartap hydrochloride 4G @ 25 kg/ha.
- **Rice Blast (*Magnaporthe oryzae*)**:
  - Symptoms: Spindle-shaped lesions with grayish centers and dark brown margins.
  - Control: Avoid excess urea. Spray Tricyclazole 75% WP @ 0.6 g/L of water at the first sign of leaf lesions.
