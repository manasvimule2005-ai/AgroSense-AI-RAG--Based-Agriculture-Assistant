# FAO & ICAR Guidelines: Soil Health Card Standards, Testing & Interpretation

*Document Source: Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare (Soil Health Card Scheme) & FAO Soil Fertility Management Manual.*

## 1. Primary Soil Parameters & Critical Benchmark Ranges

Soil testing evaluates nutrient availability and chemical characteristics to prevent under-fertilization or toxic over-fertilization.

| Parameter | Unit | Low / Deficient | Medium / Optimal | High / Excessive | Corrective Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **pH (Reaction)** | - | < 6.0 (Acidic) | 6.5 - 7.5 (Neutral) | > 8.5 (Alkaline/Sodic) | Acidic: Apply Lime (CaCO3). Alkaline/Sodic: Apply Agricultural Gypsum (CaSO4.2H2O). |
| **Electrical Conductivity (EC)** | dS/m | < 1.0 (Non-saline) | 1.0 - 2.0 (Normal) | > 2.0 (Saline) | Leaching with fresh water; improve sub-surface drainage; organic mulching. |
| **Organic Carbon (OC)** | % | < 0.50% (Low) | 0.50% - 0.75% (Medium) | > 0.75% (High) | Apply FYM (10-15 t/ha), vermicompost (2.5 t/ha), or green manuring with *Sesbania aculeata* (Dhaincha). |
| **Available Nitrogen (N)** | kg/ha | < 280 (Low) | 280 - 560 (Medium) | > 560 (High) | Low: Increase recommended nitrogen dose by 25%; apply biofertilizers (Azotobacter / Rhizobium). |
| **Available Phosphorus (P2O5)** | kg/ha | < 23 (Low) | 23 - 56 (Medium) | > 56 (High) | Low: Apply Single Super Phosphate (SSP) or DAP; inoculate seeds with Phosphate Solubilizing Bacteria (PSB). |
| **Available Potassium (K2O)** | kg/ha | < 144 (Low) | 144 - 336 (Medium) | > 336 (High) | Low: Apply Muriate of Potash (MOP / KCl) or Potassium Sulfate (SOP) in splits. |
| **Available Sulfur (S)** | ppm | < 10.0 (Deficient) | 10.0 - 20.0 (Sufficient) | > 20.0 (High) | Apply Gypsum, Elemental Sulfur, or Bentonite sulfur (20-25 kg S/ha) for oilseeds and pulses. |
| **Zinc (Zn)** | ppm | < 0.60 (Deficient) | 0.60 - 1.20 (Adequate) | > 1.20 (High) | Apply Zinc Sulphate heptahydrate (ZnSO4.7H2O) @ 25 kg/ha once every 2 years. |

## 2. Soil pH Management and Reclamation
- **Acid Soils (pH < 6.0)**:
  - Cause: High rainfall areas leaching basic cations (Ca, Mg, K); excessive use of ammonium-based fertilizers.
  - Consequence: Aluminum and Manganese toxicity; Phosphorus fixation into insoluble Al/Fe phosphates.
  - Reclamation: Apply agricultural lime (calcium carbonate) or dolomite based on lime requirement tests. Broadcast and incorporate thoroughly during preparatory tillage 3-4 weeks prior to planting.
- **Sodic / Alkaline Soils (pH > 8.5, ESP > 15%)**:
  - Cause: Accumulation of exchangeable sodium carbonates/bicarbonates causing soil dispersion and poor aeration.
  - Consequence: Severe crusting, poor drainage, root asphyxiation, zinc and iron chlorosis.
  - Reclamation: Incorporate agricultural gypsum (*Calcium Sulphate*) followed by heavy ponding and leaching to displace sodium with calcium. Grow tolerant green manure crops like Dhaincha (*Sesbania*).

## 3. Integrated Nutrient Management (INM)
INM combines inorganic chemical fertilizers with organic manures and microbial inoculants:
1. **Biofertilizers**:
   - *Rhizobium*: Species-specific for legumes; fixes 50-100 kg N/ha.
   - *Azotobacter / Azospirillum*: Free-living/associative N-fixers for cereals; supplements 15-20 kg N/ha.
   - *Phosphate Solubilizing Bacteria (PSB)*: Solubilizes insoluble rock phosphates and soil-bound phosphorus.
2. **Organic Manures**:
   - Farmyard Manure (FYM): Enhances soil water holding capacity, cation exchange capacity (CEC), and biological activity.
   - Vermicompost: High in plant-available micronutrients and growth hormones.
